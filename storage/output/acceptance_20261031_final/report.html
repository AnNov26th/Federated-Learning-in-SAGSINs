SAGSIN: held-out stress matrix

Frozen normal-trained models; mixed/congested are distribution shifts. Decision at t, scoring at t+1. Shared link/node/access capacity. 0.1 s setup/handover loss. Capacity scale represents residual reservations; it does not change synthetic delay labels. Outage removes a deterministic ~25% of undirected contacts during the middle third. Latency is max of both directions, conditional on availability. QoS and delivery include failures. Paired delta = FL minus baseline. Lower MAE/delay and higher delivery/QoS are better. 95% percentile bootstrap resamples seeds (5000 draws); five seeds are exploratory, without multiple-comparison correction. No packet/RF/media or field QoS claim. Communication cost belongs to training report, not this offline replay.
